# Launch plan: the first 16 long videos (your list)

**Style for every video:** Open with the big economic story in two or three sentences (what changed nationally, with the official number), then pivot fast: 'here's what that means for YOUR money'. Spend most of the video on concrete steps for a 25-45-year-old who is stretched thin. Free options first. 

**How the numbers are checked:**
- Every number below was checked on the official page on 2026-11-19.
- Before each video is announced, the planner opens the page again. If the number is gone (it may have
  changed), the fact is **dropped** from the video and the announcement says so.
- Some agencies block automated readers (FTC, sometimes ED.gov). For those pages the fact stays as checked
  and is flagged in the announcement so you can confirm it.
- Re-check everything at any time: `python -m glivarao verify-facts`.

**Rules:** the list runs in this order, with deadline topics first. It overrides the pillar and news rules;
those (60% debt and credit, at most 1 news video in 4) start with video 17.

## Notes from the fact check
- **2026 vs 2027 standard deduction:** the IRS page "Credits and deductions for individuals" labels **$16,600**
  as the 2026 amount, but the IRS's own release (IR-2026-133) shows $16,600 is the **2027** amount. Returns filed in
  2027 (tax year 2026) use **$16,100** (single) and **$32,200** (married filing jointly). The videos use the
  correct 2026 numbers.
- **SAVE:** StudentAid.gov says a court order ended SAVE "in March 2026". Each borrower's 90-day deadline
  depends on their servicer's notice, so the video tells viewers to check their own notice.
- **ACA:** HealthCare.gov confirms the 100%–400% FPL rule and the December 15 / January 15 dates. State
  marketplaces can have different dates; the videos say so.
