Here's a number most people never see on their credit card statement.

Say you owe $5,000 on a card with a 24% APR, and you only make a typical minimum payment. You'd be paying for nearly 20 years. The interest alone? About $8,900. *More than you borrowed in the first place.*

Now change just one thing. Pay a steady $200 a month instead. The balance is gone in about 3 years, with roughly $2,000 in interest.

And check every charge on that statement. In the last three months, US consumers filed more than 8,000 complaints with the CFPB about purchases on their statements. That's up 58% from a year earlier. If something looks wrong, dispute it quickly, and in writing.
